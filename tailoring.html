Tailoring Training Programme

Are you interested in becoming a professional fashion designer or tailor? Leelbeey General Services offers a practical tailoring training programme for beginners and aspiring fashion designers.

Our training is hands-on and structured to help you build real, marketable skills. You will learn everything from the basics of sewing to constructing complete garments.

Whether you want to start your own tailoring business, enhance an existing skill, or simply learn a valuable trade, our training is designed for you.

What You Will Learn

  • Sewing machine operation & techniques
  • Fabric selection & preparation
  • Taking & working with measurements
  • Pattern-making fundamentals
  • Women's clothing construction
  • Children's and baby clothing
  • Fashion design basics
