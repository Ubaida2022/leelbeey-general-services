Our Story

A Growing Brand Built on Trust & Quality

Leelbeey General Services is a growing multi-service business based in Zaria, Kaduna State, Nigeria. We combine passion, professionalism, and creativity to serve our customers across a wide range of everyday needs.

Our business spans professional tailoring and fashion design, tailoring training for aspiring designers, quality textiles and fashion products, kitchen utensils, web development, and graphic design — all under one trusted brand.

We believe that quality service should be accessible. Whether you are looking for a beautifully crafted outfit, quality Ankara fabric, reliable kitchen essentials, or a professional website, Leelbeey General Services is here to help.

We are committed to building lasting relationships with our customers through reliability, creativity, and excellent service.
